# Manhattan coffee research notes

Researched October 2026. The brief: a chocolatey, rich coffee shop in Manhattan with excellent beans and a unique experience.

## How shops were scored

Each shop gets a 1–5 rating on three criteria. The fit score in the app is the average of the three.

| Criterion | What earns a 5 |
|---|---|
| Chocolatey & rich | Published tasting notes of chocolate or cacao, a full body, or a chocolate signature drink |
| Bean quality | Traceable sourcing, in-house or own-farm roasting, freshness, named producers |
| Unique experience | Something you can't get at other NYC cafés: the room, the brew method, or the story |

### 1. Devoción, 25 E 20th St

- **Beans:** Roasts "true farm-fresh coffee only 10 days after leaving origin" (the industry norm is 6–12 months). Buys from independent Colombian producers, dry-mills in Bogotá and ships by FedEx to its NYC roaster. [devocion.com](https://www.devocion.com/pages/devocion-cafe-flatiron-district-new-york-ny)
- **Chocolate:** The Toro House Blend, built for espresso, lists cacao, vanilla, cherry and almond. [Bean Box](https://beanbox.com/coffee/roast/devocion-toro/1356)
- **Experience:** A restored, landmarked artist's studio with exposed brick, a skylight over the bar and a plant-filled atrium. [Brian's Coffee Spot](https://www.brian-coffee-spot.com/2022/10/29/devocion-flatiron-district/)
- **Note:** A smaller second café opened in 2025 at 1134 Broadway (NoMad). [Flatiron NoMad Partnership](https://flatironnomad.nyc/2025/04/30/new-neighbor-roundup-devocion-opto-skinny-louie/)
- **Order:** Espresso or a cortado on Toro.

### 2. 787 Coffee, 131 E 7th St

- **Beans:** Grown, hand-picked and roasted from the owners' farm, Hacienda Iluminada, at about 3,000 ft in Maricao, Puerto Rico. [787 Coffee](https://www.787coffee.com/blog/coffee-farm-tour-puerto-rico-beans)
- **Chocolate:** "Notes of rich dark chocolate, comforting molasses, and a hint of toasted almond". Low acidity and a rich body. [787 Coffee](https://www.787coffee.com/blog/how-to-taste-puerto-rican-coffee-farm-to-cup)
- **Experience:** Rum-, whiskey- and tequila-infused beans, each listing dark chocolate among its notes. Swing seats and a patio. Opened October 2018 as the brand's first shop. [787 shop](https://www.787coffee.com/787coffeeonlineshop/p/787coffeeinfusedcoffeetrio), [EV Grieve](https://evgrieve.com/2018/08/787-coffee-for-7th-street.html)
- **Order:** A cortado on the house single origin. Take home a bag of the rum-infused beans.

### 3. Felix Roasting Co., 450 Park Ave S

- **Chocolate:** The hickory-smoked s'mores latte combines graham-cracker-infused milk, espresso, dark chocolate sauce and house-made marshmallow, smoked under a glass bell. [One Girl Whole World](https://onegirlwholeworld.com/north-america/felix-roasting-co-nyc/)
- **Experience:** Interior inspired by the Aesthetic Movement, with a 500-tile hand-drawn mural and a bespoke terrazzo floor. [Daily Coffee News](https://dailycoffeenews.com/2020/02/14/the-kaleidoscopic-coffee-opulence-of-felix-roasting-co-in-manhattan)
- **Beans:** Roasts its own coffee on a 12-kilo Probat, but the roastery is in Houston. That's why it scores 3 on beans. [Sprudge](https://sprudge.com/get-lost-in-the-elegance-at-new-york-citys-felix-roasting-co-138068.html)
- **Status:** Yelp (July 2026) lists the Park Ave S flagship as open, Mon–Fri 8–5 and Sat 8–6. The Greene St (SoHo) shop is closed. [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/felix-roasting-new-york-4)

### 4. Café Integral, 149 Elizabeth St

- **Beans:** Serves only Nicaraguan coffee, roasted in house. Founded in 2012 by Nicaraguan-born Cesar Vega. [Daily Coffee News](https://dailycoffeenews.com/2016/09/21/with-nicaraguan-coffee-focus-cafe-integral-expanding-through-the-us)
- **Chocolate:** The Dulcinea espresso (La Argentina, Dipilto, and El Bosque, San Fernando) lists "nutty dark chocolate", hazelnut and red cherry. [The Way to Coffee](https://www.thewaytocoffee.com/beans/dulcinea/)
- **Status:** Yelp (September 2026) lists it as open daily 8–4. [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/caf%C3%A9-integral-new-york-2)

### 5. Hi-Collar, 231 E 9th St

- **Experience:** A Japanese kissaten. Every cup is brewed by hand by pour-over, AeroPress or a gold siphon, and served in a Christofle gold-faced cup. About a dozen seats. Becomes a sake and whisky bar at night. [Sprudge](https://sprudge.com/bernson-hi-collar-40077.html), [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/restaurants/hi-collar)
- **Beans:** Limited, rotating roasts from Bali, Colombia and Kenya.
- **Address:** Hi-Collar moved from 10th St to 231 E 9th St in 2020, and older guides still list the old address. [Yelp, 2026](https://www.yelp.com/biz/hi-collar-new-york-2)
- **Caveat:** There are no published chocolate tasting notes, so it scores 3 on chocolate. Ask the barista for the most chocolate-leaning bean.

## Near misses

- **Abraço, 86 E 7th St:** The Infatuation describes "biting, chocolate-y espresso" and a famous olive oil cake. Less information on its beans. [Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/new-york/reviews/abraco)
- **MarieBelle Cacao Bar, 484 Broome St:** The most chocolate of any shop here (Aztec hot chocolate under a chandelier), but it's a chocolatier first. [MarieBelle](https://mariebelle.com/pages/cacao-bar)
- **Caffè Reggio, 119 MacDougal St:** Opened in 1927, credited with introducing cappuccino to the US, and has a 1902 espresso machine. Strong on history, but not a specialty roaster. [Untapped Cities](https://untappedcities.com/the-oldest-espresso-machine-in-nyc-is-in-cafe-reggio)
- **Considered and left out:** La Cabra and SEY (light Nordic roasts are fruit-forward, not chocolatey, and SEY is in Brooklyn), Coffee Project NY (its three-glass deconstructed latte is unique, but there was no chocolate evidence), Black Fox and Joe Coffee.

## Limits of this research

- Evidence comes from web search results. Most source pages couldn't be opened directly from the research environment, so quotes are as reported in search summaries.
- Hours and open status come from 2026 listings. Check before you go.
- Map pins are placed by address and may be off by a few doors. The Street View angles are estimates, and you can pan the view.
